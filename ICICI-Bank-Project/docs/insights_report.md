# Insights: ICICI Bank, FY2004–FY2025

All figures come from RBI data and are reproduced by `sql/02_analysis_queries.sql` and `excel/ICICI_Bank_Analysis.xlsx`.

## 1. The bad-loan cycle
- **FY04–FY08: low NPAs.** The GNPA ratio fell to **1.51% in FY06** while loans grew 35–60% a year.
- **FY09–FY11: first stress.** The ratio rose to **6.52% in FY10**, after the global financial crisis.
- **FY16–FY19: the big stress.** The ratio peaked at **9.90% in FY18**. This matches the Indian banking sector's corporate bad-loan problem and RBI's 2015 Asset Quality Review, which made banks recognise hidden bad loans.
- **FY19–FY25: clean-up.** The ratio fell every year to **1.73% in FY25**, the lowest since FY06. Gross NPAs fell from ₹53,240 cr to ₹23,523 cr (**−56%**).

## 2. Growth
Gross advances grew from ₹63,823 cr (FY04) to ₹13,59,790 cr (FY25), which is **21.3×**, or about **15.7% a year**. Growth has stayed between 9% and 18% a year since FY19, even while NPAs were being cleaned up.

## 3. Profit vs provisions
- Provisions as a share of operating profit peaked at **85.7% in FY19**: almost all of the bank's operating profit went into covering bad loans.
- By **FY24–FY25 it was about 30%**. Operating profit rose from ₹23,438 cr (FY19) to ₹67,299 cr (FY25), so profit after provisions grew **14×** (₹3,363 cr to ₹47,227 cr).
- The stress years, when provisions were more than 50% of operating profit, were FY09–FY10 and FY16–FY21.

## 4. NPA build-up vs clean-up
- **FY17** had the largest build-up: ₹25,969 cr of new NPAs, against ₹15,912 cr recovered or written off.
- **FY19 and FY20** were the only years in which clean-up was larger than new NPAs.
- Recoveries plus write-offs reached a high of **₹16,782 cr in FY25**.

## 5. Operational risk and customer service
- **Fraud:** the amount was under ₹10 cr in every year from FY18 to FY22, then jumped to **₹322.6 cr in FY24**, with 6,588 incidents. The average size per incident rose from about ₹0.2–0.3 lakh to **₹4.9 lakh**. This is worth investigating further.
- **Complaints:** Ombudsman complaints doubled from 11,257 (FY19) to 21,372 (FY20) and peaked at **28,065 in FY21**, during COVID's shift to digital banking, then fell to 16,602 (FY23).

## 6. Risk exposure
- Exposure to sensitive sectors (capital markets, real estate, commodities) grew to ₹6.57 lakh cr, but stayed between about **0.4× and 0.6× gross advances** throughout. It grew in line with the loan book.

## Recommendations (as an analyst)
1. Keep tracking new NPA additions, which are still about ₹20,000 cr a year, even though the GNPA ratio is low.
2. Investigate the FY24 fraud spike: which product or channel caused it?
3. Use the FY21 complaints peak to improve digital service, and monitor complaint resolution rates.
